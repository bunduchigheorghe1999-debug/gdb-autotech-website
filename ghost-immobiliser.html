Ghost Immobiliser

A discreet vehicle immobiliser designed to help protect against key cloning, relay theft and other unauthorised attempts to drive the vehicle.

Why choose Ghost-II?

  • Discreet CANbus integration with no visible hardware.
  • Uses a unique disarm sequence entered through the vehicle's existing buttons.
  • No additional key fob or radio signal is required for the standard PIN operation.
  • Designed to help protect against key cloning, relay theft, hot-wiring and OBD-related attacks.
  • Service / Valet mode is available for workshops and vehicle handovers.
  • The disarm sequence can be set and changed after installation.

How it works

  • Switch the ignition on.
  • Enter your personal disarm sequence using the vehicle's existing buttons.
  • Once authorised, the vehicle can be started and driven normally.
  • The system remains discreetly integrated into the vehicle.

Important for customers

  • Vehicle compatibility should be checked before booking.
  • GDB Autotech can advise on compatibility and installation for your vehicle.
