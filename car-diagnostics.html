Car Diagnostics

Professional diagnostic scanning to help identify warning lights, fault codes and electronic system issues before you spend money on unnecessary parts.

What we can help with

  • Engine warning lights and stored fault codes.
  • Electronic and communication faults.
  • Initial investigation of ABS, airbag and other vehicle system warnings where supported by the diagnostic equipment.
  • Pre-repair fault scanning and post-repair checks.
  • Clear explanation of the diagnostic findings and recommended next steps.

Why diagnostics first?

  • A warning light does not always identify the exact failed component.
  • Scanning the vehicle can help narrow down the cause before parts are replaced.
  • Diagnostics can be useful before buying a used vehicle or when a fault keeps returning.

Important for customers

  • A diagnostic scan identifies stored information and helps with fault-finding; it does not automatically mean a component needs replacing.
  • Further testing or mechanical repair may be required depending on the fault found.
